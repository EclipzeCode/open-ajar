Right now, opportunity is scattered.

It lives in counselor emails, university websites, company career pages, nonprofit newsletters, Instagram, LinkedIn, and conversations with parents and coaches.

If you’re well connected, some of it reaches you. If you’re not, most of it never does. Open Ajar puts it in one place.

How it works

From finding it to walking through.

  1. Discover

    Search and filter by interest, grade, state, cost and deadline.

  2. Evaluate

    See eligibility, cost or pay, requirements, and how well we’ve checked it.

  3. Track

    Save it, turn its requirements into a checklist and add your own notes.

  4. Apply

    Go straight to the organization’s real application. We never sit in the middle.
