Questions & answers

Still wondering? Email {{ contact_email }}.

What is OpenAjar?

A student-founded site where high-school students find internships, research and summer programs, scholarships, competitions and teen jobs in one place, then track them through to applying.

Is it free?

Yes. OpenAjar is free for students, always. We never charge to browse, save or track anything.

Do I apply through OpenAjar?

No. Every listing links to the organization’s own application. We help you find it and stay organized; you apply directly with them.

How do you check listings?

Nothing goes live automatically. A reviewer checks each listing against its official page first, and each one is labeled with how well it’s been verified. Always confirm details on the official page before you apply.

Can I share an opportunity I know about?

Please do. Create a free account, confirm your email, and use Submit. A reviewer will approve it or let you know what to fix.

Is my information private?

We only collect what the site needs, never sell it and don’t run ads. You can delete your account any time. See the privacy policy.
