OpenAjar

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

OpenAjar is a student-founded platform that helps high-school students find and track opportunities. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we do with it. If anything is unclear, email {{ contact_email }}.

Who we are

OpenAjar is run by a team of students. You can reach us at {{ contact_email }}.

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your password, stored only as a scrambled (hashed) value that we cannot read.
  • Optional profile details you choose to add: your school, grade, interests and general location.

As you use OpenAjar, we store the opportunities you save and track, along with any notes, deadlines and checklist items you add, and, if you tell us, whether you found an opportunity you were accepted to on OpenAjar. We also record which days you use the site while signed in. We only use that and your school in overall counts (for example, how many students use OpenAjar each month and at how many schools), never to show anything about you publicly.

If you submit an opportunity, we collect the listing details and a contact email so reviewers can follow up. Your contact email is never shown publicly.

If you apply to intern with us, we collect what you put in the application so we can match you with a business. If we place you, we record where, when you start, your hours and your supervisor, and show them on your account page. The business you’re placed with sees your first name and schedule on its own account page.

To help us support you, the OpenAjar team can keep a nickname and short notes on your account. Only the team sees them; they’re never shown to you or anyone else, and they’re deleted with your account.

If you ask for an Access Grant, we collect the program details, the amount, why you’re asking and a parent or guardian’s email. Only the OpenAjar team sees your request. We email our decision to you and to that parent or guardian, and we may contact the program to pay it.

To keep you signed in, we use one secure, signed cookie. It is necessary for the site to work. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, we do not run ads, and we do not sell your information.

Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To keep your saved and tracked opportunities in one place across your devices.
  • To send account emails (confirming your address, resetting your password) and, if you leave them on, deadline reminders for opportunities you track.
  • To review opportunities people submit before they appear to students, and to place interns.

Who we share it with

Only the services that make OpenAjar work, and only what they need:

  • Our email provider (Resend) receives your email address and the contents of emails we send you.
  • Our hosting provider (Render) stores the site and its database on our behalf.

How we protect it

Passwords are hashed with a strong algorithm and never stored in readable form. The site is served over HTTPS and the sign-in cookie is marked HttpOnly and Secure. No system is perfectly secure, but we follow sensible practices and fix problems quickly.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from your account page, which permanently removes your profile, saved opportunities and tracked applications. You can also email us and we’ll delete it for you. Access Grant requests and payment records are kept after an account is deleted, so we can account for every grant. Internship applications, messages you send through our contact form and partner inquiries aren’t tied to your account (you can send them without one), so they stay after you delete it; email us and we’ll delete them too.

Students and parents

OpenAjar is for high-school students who are 13 or older. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you are under 18, please use OpenAjar with a parent or guardian’s awareness. Parents who believe their child gave us information can email us and we’ll remove it.

Your choices

You can update your profile, turn off reminder emails, remove saved or tracked opportunities, or delete your account any time from your account page.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we’ll try to let signed-in users know.
