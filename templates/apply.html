Become an intern

Apply to intern with Open Ajar.

We place motivated high-school students as interns with local businesses we work with. Tell us about yourself and we’ll be in touch when there’s a fit. No account needed.

How it works

  1. You apply here. It takes a few minutes.
  2. We get to know you and what you’re looking for.
  3. When a business we work with needs an intern who fits, we introduce you.
  4. You get real experience. They get a hand.

Applying doesn’t guarantee a placement. We reach out as opportunities come up.
