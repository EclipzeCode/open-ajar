Why we exist

Your next opportunity shouldn’t depend on who you know.

Opportunities live in counselor emails, university sites, career pages, nonprofit newsletters, Instagram, LinkedIn, and conversations with parents and coaches. Students with strong networks tend to hear about the good ones. Students without those connections can miss them entirely.

OpenAjar is a student-founded attempt to change that: one organized place to find opportunities, understand them, and follow them through.

Why “ajar”

An ajar door isn’t completely open, but it’s open enough for you to see that something is on the other side.

That’s the idea. Opportunity shouldn’t be hidden behind connections or luck. We make the door easier to find and give you the tools to walk through it.

Access Grants

Sometimes the door is found, but a fee stands in front of it. Access Grants cover up to ${{ D.GRANT_CAP }} a year per student toward application fees, program fees, tests and materials.

Students tell us about the program and why it matters to them, and someone on our team reviews every request. If we approve it, we pay the program directly, never the student, and donations fund every grant.

How listings get here

Nothing is published automatically. Every listing, whether a person submitted it or we imported it, waits in a review queue until an admin approves it, asks for changes, or declines it. You always apply on the organization’s own site.

  • VerifiedA reviewer checked the official page recently.
  • Source-linkedApproved by a reviewer. Details come from the organization’s page and may have changed.
  • UnreviewedNot yet independently checked.

The longer view

Students who benefit from OpenAjar can help other students discover opportunities, so access compounds instead of staying with the people who already have it.
