What support makes possible

Where the money goes.

  1. Keep OpenAjar free

    Students never pay to use OpenAjar. Contributions cover what it costs to run, so money is never the reason a student misses out.

  2. Find and verify more

    We add and check internships, programs, scholarships and competitions, and confirm each links to a real application.

  3. Place students as interns

    We reach out to local businesses, introduce them to motivated students, and help set up real, hands-on internships.

  4. Reach those who need it most

    We get OpenAjar in front of students who don’t already have someone pointing them toward opportunities.

These describe how support is put to use, not a line-item breakdown. OpenAjar tracks funds raised and used so support goes directly to expanding student access.
