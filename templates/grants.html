Access Grants

Don’t let a fee close the door.

Found a program you want to do, but the fee is in the way? Ask us to cover it. Access Grants pay up to ${{ D.GRANT_CAP }} a year per student toward application fees, program fees, tests and materials.

  1. Tell us about it

    What the program is, what it costs, and why it matters to you. A parent or guardian should know you’re asking.

  2. We review it

    Someone on the team reads every request, and may email you with a question or ask for your acceptance letter or invoice.

  3. We pay the program

    If we approve it, we pay the program directly, never you, and email you and your parent or guardian once it’s done.

Before you ask

Many programs waive their own fees if you ask, and that can cover more than we can. Check the program’s financial aid page or email them first.

Applying to college? The Common App, NACAC and College Board all have application fee waivers for students who qualify. Your school counselor can help you use them.

Grants depend on the funds we have and aren’t guaranteed. One open request at a time.
